Campaign

Pairs of papers are scored, the strongest are researched by two peers and an independent verifier, and the accepted accounts are edited into readable notes and papers.

Scan

Each pair of papers is read once by a model, which proposes a connexion and scores its feasibility and its gain. Pairs rank by feasibility × gain; the shortlist is a cut of this ranking.
