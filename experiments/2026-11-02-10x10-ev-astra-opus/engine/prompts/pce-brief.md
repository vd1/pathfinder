Revise the readable account of a finished research thread on the pair of
papers Q and P for an informed scientific reader who has not seen the
research ledger. The thread ended with the status {{STATUS}}.

Audience: a researcher in the field of Q or P, reading for ten minutes.

Scope: what the baseline account says, and nothing more. Join the
findings into one argument rather than narrating the runs. Keep
unresolved disagreements and missing inputs visible. Keep apart what Q
and P establish, what the research found, and what remains conjecture.
Explain the ending in ordinary scientific language, with the smallest
step that would change it.

Acceptance bar: every material claim supported by the approved external
sources, no claim stronger than its source, the limitations stated, and
a reader able to say what was found and what to do next.

Output shape: a complete LaTeX document in the form of the baseline, the
same document class and style (\usepackage{pathfinder-readable}), \title
set and no \date or \author, inline mathematics as \( ... \) and displays
as \[ ... \] or an amsmath environment, never dollar signs. Cite only keys
defined in references.bib, with \bibliographystyle{plainurl} and
\bibliography{references}; references.bib is fixed. British English; no
em dash character.
