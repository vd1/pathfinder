\section*{A cost-aware robustness target for MARS-RA}

\paragraph{What the present theorem controls.}
Let \(v_t^*\) denote task-grounded credit, for example a Shapley vector, and
let \(c_t^*\) denote the latent Bradley--Terry parameter of the comparator.
The triangle inequality gives
\[
 \lVert \widehat c_t-v_t^*\rVert_2
 \leq
 \lVert \widehat c_t-c_t^*\rVert_2
 +\lVert c_t^*-v_t^*\rVert_2.
\]
Proposition 1 of P controls only the first term, under correct specification
and connectivity.  Repeated queries can reduce this sampling term but not the
second, semantic-bias term.  Proposition 2 sets the second term to zero as an
assumption.

\paragraph{Why asymptotic policy quality is the wrong target.}
For P's shaping reward
\[
 F_t=\gamma\psi_{t+1}-\psi_t,
\]
the discounted shaping return over an episode is
\[
 \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\gamma^tF_t
 =-\psi_0+\gamma^T\psi_T.
\]
P sets the terminal potential to zero, so this is \(-\psi_0\).  With a fixed
initial state, the shift is independent of the subsequent policy.  Arbitrarily
bad comparisons therefore need not change the exactly optimized policy
objective.  They can still change optimization speed and finite-sample
behavior.  A nontrivial robustness result must consequently concern regret,
sample complexity, or performance after a fixed training budget.

\paragraph{A concrete frontier to prove.}
Suppose a finite-training analysis establishes that credit perturbation enters
with sensitivity \(L\), and write
\[
 \epsilon_K\leq b+A K^{-1/2},
\]
where \(b\) is semantic bias and \(A\) contains dimension, confidence, and
comparison-graph dependence.  Charging \(\kappa\) per comparison, the part of
an upper bound controlled by the query count would be
\[
 Lb+LAK^{-1/2}+\kappa K.
\]
Its continuous minimizer is
\[
 K^*=\left(\frac{LA}{2\kappa}\right)^{2/3}.
\]
This supplies a falsifiable query-allocation law and predicts a bias floor:
increasing \(K\) has diminishing returns and cannot repair systematically
wrong comparisons.  The missing result is the sensitivity theorem that
connects \(\epsilon_K\) to finite-training MARL performance.

\paragraph{Assumptions that must be made explicit.}
The comparison outcomes must be independent or have a declared dependence
model, the Bradley--Terry link must be correctly specified or assigned a
misspecification residual, and graph connectivity must be quantified through
its spectral gap.  Position-reversed calls to one deterministic LMM are not
automatically independent Bradley--Terry samples.  Also, P states different
normalizations of its error rate in the proposition and appendix restatement;
that rate should be repaired before it is propagated.
