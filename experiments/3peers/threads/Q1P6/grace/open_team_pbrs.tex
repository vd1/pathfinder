\section*{Open-team PBRS audit}

Let \(\mathcal N=\{1,\ldots,n\}\) be the fixed population and let
\(I^t\subseteq\mathcal N\) be the active coalition. Paper P estimates
\(\hat{\boldsymbol c}_t\in\mathbb R^{|I^t|}\), then sets
\(\psi_t=\operatorname{softmax}(\hat{\boldsymbol c}_t)\) and writes
\(F_t=\gamma\psi_{t+1}-\psi_t\). If \(I^{t+1}\ne I^t\), the subtraction
is not defined without maps from the active-set spaces into a common codomain.
There is also a scalar-vector mismatch between P's scalar environmental reward
and its vector-valued potential.

A fixed-population repair chooses \(\Phi_t\in\mathbb R^n\), for example
\[
  \Phi_{t,i}=\mathbf 1\{i\in I^t\}p_{t,i},
\]
with a declared convention for inactive coordinates. If every coordinate
receives the shaping transition
\[
  F_{t,i}=\gamma\Phi_{t+1,i}-\Phi_{t,i}
\]
at every time step, then
\[
  \sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\gamma^tF_{t,i}
  =-\Phi_{0,i}+\gamma^T\Phi_{T,i}.
\]
Thus zero terminal potential and a policy-independent initial potential recover
the usual objective invariance, regardless of comparison accuracy.

If instead coordinate \(i\) is shaped only while active, consider an active
interval \([\tau_i,\sigma_i)\). The within-interval sum is
\[
  \sum_{t=\tau_i}^{\sigma_i-1}\gamma^tF_{t,i}
  =-\gamma^{\tau_i}\Phi_{	au_i,i}
   +\gamma^{\sigma_i}\Phi_{sigma_i,i}.
\]
These entry and exit terms need not be policy-independent because coalition
transitions are part of P's dynamics. Omitting transitions across inactivity
therefore does not inherit the standard PBRS theorem. Including them restores
telescoping but can create large boundary shaping signals whose effect on
finite-training optimization remains open.

This changes the joint research question. The first theorem should characterize
the necessary typing and boundary conditions for policy invariance in an open
Dec-POMDP. Only after that repair should comparison error be decomposed into a
finite-query term and semantic bias. A corrected constrained or regularized
Bradley--Terry estimator could support
\[
  \inf_{a\in\mathbb R}
  \frac{\|\hat{\boldsymbol c}_t-(\boldsymbol v_t^*+a\mathbf 1)\|_2}{\sqrt n}
  \le b_t+A K^{-1/2},
\]
where \(b_t\) is misspecification or semantic bias. P's stated unregularized
MLE does not supply this result under connectivity alone because complete
separation can prevent a finite optimizer.

The ordinary optimal-return consistency versus robustness curve is either
undefined under P's displayed active-set construction or vacuous after the
fixed-population repair. The nontrivial downstream target is finite-training
regret or sample complexity, including comparison cost. If a learner-specific
sensitivity bound \(L\) is established, the conditional objective
\[
  Lb+LAK^{-1/2}+\kappa K
\]
has continuous minimizer
\[
  K^*=\left(\frac{LA}{2\kappa}\right)^{2/3}.
\]
Without such a sensitivity theorem, this frontier is a research target rather
than a consequence of either paper.
