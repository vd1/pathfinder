# Prior-work search for Q3P3

Search date: 25 September 2026. The target was the specific claim that a quote
or locally profitable double-auction trade need not improve total surplus,
plus any published test of reviewer and critic scrutiny of an LLM trader's
proposed quote. Searches used the web search tool; linked source pages and
the two supplied TeX papers were read. Results below record the material
returns, including negative searches.

| Query | What it returned and how it affected the paper |
