# Prior-work search for the quote-ranking result

Searched on 27 September 2026. The target was a pairwise judgment of two
quotes by final welfare under private values, especially a fixed-value-schedule
example with identical acting-trader information and opposite realised welfare
rankings. The verdict concerns this target, not the wider auction or credit
assignment literature. % ledger: 11, 16, 19, 20, 22

## Queries and returns

1. `"private values" "pairwise" "welfare" "double auction" counterfactual action`
   returned general private-value and market-credit material, including a
   peer-to-peer energy-trading credit paper. No retrieved hit gave the stated
   quote-ranking construction.
2. `"observationally identical" "welfare" "auction" hidden values` returned
   [Self-confirming policies](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11238-021-09862-9).
   That work concerns feedback, beliefs, and expected payoffs; it does not give
   the Q-specific two-quote construction. It reinforces that this paper should
   present a conditional instance, not a general priority claim.
3. `"pairwise" "quote" "welfare" "double auction" LLM` returned auction and
   pricing pages, but no retrieved paper with pairwise quote labels under Q's
   information set.
4. `"counterfactual" "welfare" "credit assignment" "double auction"` returned
   adjacent counterfactual-credit and market papers, without the construction.
5. `site:arxiv.org/abs/ "private" "welfare" "pairwise" "double auction"` returned
   no specific matching paper.
6. `site:arxiv.org/abs/ "partial observability" "pairwise" "credit assignment" welfare`
   returned no matching private-value market result.
7. `site:arxiv.org/abs/ "counterfactual" "action" "auction" "welfare" LLM`
   returned no matching quote-ranking result.
8. `"Exact Is Easier" "alternative" "restored" "credit"` returned
   [Exact Is Easier](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06859), which already uses
   restored decision points and alternative-action rollouts in cooperative LLM
   systems. The paper explicitly credits that method step. % ledger: 16

## Papers read and effect on the draft

I read the arXiv abstract pages for
[Q](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02580),
[P](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27967),
[Exact Is Easier](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06859),
[FairMarket-RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18610), and
[Shapley Counterfactual Credits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00285). I also
checked the [C3 method section](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.06859v2)
and the [FairMarket-RL paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.18610) for the
specific rollout and auction-intervention claims. The last
already addresses counterfactual credit in cooperative learning; it was not
cited because the more specific alternative-action study carries the relevant
methodological point. FairMarket-RL already uses an LLM critic to shape bidding
in a continuous double auction, so this draft does not claim an LLM-guided
auction intervention as new. The retrieved works did not state the
fixed-schedule, two-world numerical result; this search does not prove
priority. % ledger: 7, 16, 17, 23
