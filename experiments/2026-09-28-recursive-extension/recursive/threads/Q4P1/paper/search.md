I read the relevant sections of [Chen, Feng and Yu](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.23243v1):
they establish truthful comparison elicitation under Bayesian strong stochastic
transitivity, and their Bradley--Terry example uses independent agent signals.
They do not state the full-report-law alias or the edge-specific reachability
criterion studied here. This establishes overlap in the elicitation step; it
does not establish novelty of the remaining formulation. I also read the
[Miller et al. DOI abstract](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.1050.0379) and the
[Shnayder et al. arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.03151): peer scoring
for truthful reports and the uninformative-equilibrium concern are prior work.
