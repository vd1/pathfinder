28 September 2026. This is an unblinded supervisor assessment, not an
external referee report or exhaustive literature novelty search.

## Outcome and interpretation

The four-thread batch completed in 113.50 minutes. Both recursive threads
and control Q1P1 produced internally accepted papers; control Q4P6 ended
PAUSE with a completed readable edit. All 34 model calls completed.
The 23 Astra audits required no recovery. Recorded research cost was
15.6002176 API-equivalent dollars, excluding supervision; no budget was
exhausted. These counts describe execution, not recursive superiority.

The useful evidence is a traceable extension of an intermediate result.
The ranking branch extends constant shared noise to edge-specific noise and
adds a restricted strategic implementation. The risk branch extends a
deterministic error-box test to a realised-data certificate and a payment-cap
comparison. Neither proves that a repeat could never discover the result.
Both blocks were given explicit, identical starting questions across arms,
so this is question-conditioned continuation rather than unguided discovery.

## Mathematical checks

`notes/check_extension_claims.py` is model-free and leaves campaign files alone.

- All 543 labelled acyclic directed graphs on four vertices were enumerated.
  Their forced pair orders match reachability; 774 incomparable-pair cases
  admit opposite linear extensions with the same positive edge means.
- Exact rational arithmetic checks the two-observer conditional-mean formula
  and strict proper-score advantage on nondegenerate test instances.
- All 512 corners of a three-action confidence box satisfy the edge inflation,
  path-potential feasibility and claimed reward-cap comparison.
- The new U/2 comparator example and the September 11 wrong-optimiser example
  reproduce exactly.

Proof review also checked the arbitrary-graph linear-extension argument,
the CVaR/CDF Lipschitz step and union bound, the negative-cycle sign convention,
the simple-path cap bound, and the control's Markov/fallback argument.
No substantive defect was found under the printed assumptions. Finite checks
are illustrative verification of those arguments, not proofs by enumeration
of arbitrary graph sizes or probability laws. No empirical validation follows.

## What next

The present evidence supports extension of inquiry through intermediate
results. It does not justify an automatic third generation. A useful next
scientific task would specify and price counterfactual access and an abstention
fallback, or test how an identified ranking changes an actual decision.
Either requires a declared application and benchmark; neither follows from
the current conditional theorems. No new campaign is authorized by this note.
