## Scientific question

Recursion extends inquiry; it need not replace or outperform repetition.
Look for a supported claim developed through a generated intermediate result
that was not observed in the historical campaigns or the matched controls.
Absence from finitely many repeats does not establish impossibility of discovery
by repetition. Acceptance counts and expenditure are descriptive only.
